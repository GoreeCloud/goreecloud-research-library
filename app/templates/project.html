Interpretation rules

“Supports” means one source provides corroborating evidence for another source or proposition. “Contradicts” records material conflict. “Updates” indicates later information. “Duplicates” flags substantially redundant evidence. “References” records an explicit citation or dependency. “Contextualizes” adds scope or interpretation without necessarily confirming the claim.
