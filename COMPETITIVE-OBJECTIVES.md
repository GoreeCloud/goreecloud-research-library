# GoreeCloud Research Library — Competitive Objectives

This document records benchmark and differentiation objectives. It does not claim feature parity with any named external product.

## Product objective

Build a first-party research environment that combines the useful parts of a read-it-later tool, source archive, evidence notebook, citation helper, and local knowledge base while remaining privacy-controlled, source-traceable, portable, and tightly integrated with GoreeCloud.

## Benchmark areas

The project should be evaluated against mature research and knowledge-management workflows in these areas:

- frictionless URL capture;
- quality of article/document extraction;
- PDF and technical-document usefulness;
- source metadata completeness;
- full-text search quality;
- citation interoperability;
- note/annotation ergonomics;
- change/version tracking;
- tagging, collections, saved views, and cross-source relationships;
- export and migration;
- offline/local operation;
- security of remote-content ingestion;
- privacy of browsing/research context;
- accessibility and responsive design.

## GoreeCloud differentiation targets

1. **Evidence-aware by default.** Store the epistemic status of research statements instead of treating every note as equivalent.
2. **Source history as a first-class feature.** Detect and preserve meaningful extracted-content changes.
3. **Local-first portability.** Core research must remain useful without a commercial cloud account.
4. **No mandatory external AI.** AI assistance, when implemented, should be optional and prefer approved local GoreeCloud AI capabilities.
5. **Privacy and security integration.** Privacy Shield and Wardveil should become integral research-ingestion policy systems rather than afterthoughts.
6. **Recovery integration.** Everkeep should provide verified protection of irreplaceable research state.
7. **Cross-platform graph.** GoreeCloud Mesh should allow sources, claims, projects, documents, bookmarks, notes, and other GoreeCloud objects to be related without forcing them into one monolithic database.
8. **Glaze UI consistency.** Research-heavy screens should preserve comfortable reading, clear focus, and accessible controls while still feeling native to GoreeCloud.

## Non-objectives

The project should not compete by bypassing access controls, hoarding entire third-party websites without a research need, obscuring source attribution, or locking exports into a proprietary database format.
