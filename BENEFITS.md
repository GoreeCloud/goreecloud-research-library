# GoreeCloud Research Library — Benefits

GoreeCloud Research Library is intended to improve research quality and durability without requiring a commercial hosted knowledge service.

## Supportable current benefits

- **Links become evidence records.** A URL is stored with access time, source metadata, extracted text, a content identity, and classification rather than as an uncontextualized bookmark.
- **Change is visible.** Re-fetching a source can establish whether the extracted research text changed and preserve a new revision when it did.
- **Facts and claims stay distinguishable.** The evidence model explicitly separates verified facts, source-reported claims, direct observations, tests, inferences, recommendations, decisions, assumptions, estimates, historical information, and unknowns.
- **Research remains portable.** SQLite, JSON, and CSV avoid making a hosted vendor the only keeper of the library.
- **Privacy is simpler to reason about.** The MVP has no analytics, trackers, or third-party AI processing. Research content stays in the operator-controlled database unless it is deliberately exported.
- **Source retrieval is safer than a naïve scraper.** The current fetcher includes scheme restrictions, destination validation, redirect limits, response limits, MIME restrictions, and robots checking.
- **Research can be searched later.** Extracted content and useful metadata are indexed locally.

## Intended future benefits

Future platform integrations should add authenticated multi-user use, stronger security/privacy policy enforcement, managed recovery, cross-GoreeCloud research relationships, and optional local AI assistance while preserving source-grounded evidence.

These future benefits are objectives, not claims about the current Development build.
